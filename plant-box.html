Plant Box

A concept for a plant subscription box with a companion app, made for people keeping their first plant alive.

Concept in progress!
Discipline
UI/UX and branding
Type
School concept
My part
Brand strategy, personas, interface
Tools
Figma, Illustrator

The idea

Plant Box pairs a monthly plant subscription with a companion app. The box delivers the plant. The app answers the question every beginner asks a week later: what is wrong with it?

This is a concept project. It has no customers and claims no results.

What I am making

  • Brand strategyWho the service is for and how it should sound.
  • PersonasThe beginner plant owners the concept is built around.
  • InterfaceApp screens for care guidance and diagnosis.

Good care. Less guesswork.
