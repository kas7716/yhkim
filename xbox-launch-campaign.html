Xbox Series X|S launch campaign

A gift set, posters and banners for the launch of Xbox Series X and Series S in Korea, made while I was the product manager on the brand.

Developing story!
Discipline

Context

The assignment

Xbox Series X and Series S launched in Korea while I was the product manager for Xbox at a Microsoft distributor.

A launch plan lives in spreadsheets. A launch itself lives in the things people see and hold, so alongside the planning I worked on the campaign's visual pieces.

What I made

  • Gift setA boxed set prepared for the launch.
  • PostersLaunch posters for the campaign.
  • BannersBanners carrying the campaign across channels.

Before the screen, there was the business brief.
