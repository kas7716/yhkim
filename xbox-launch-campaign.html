Graphic design and marketing, Korea, 2020 to 2021

Xbox Series X|S and the Halo Infinite gift set

A limited Halo Infinite gift set for 100 preorder customers and a sale page for Xbox Series X|S, made while I was the product manager for Xbox digital games at a Microsoft distributor in Korea.

Discipline
Graphic design, packaging, marketing
Context
Xbox team at a Microsoft distributor, Korea
My role
Product manager, Xbox C2C (digital games): gift set design and production, promotional design
Pieces
Gift box, mug, masks, item cards, sale page
Edition
100 gift sets

My role

I was the product manager for Xbox C2C, the digital game category, at a Microsoft distributor in Korea. In Korea, Xbox products reach stores through distributors. When a retailer such as Hi-Mart, Naver Store, Emart or Interpark sold a game, the code request came to us, and we requested it from Microsoft.

For each new game I registered the SKU, worked out cost and margin, and sold it on to retailers. I scheduled new releases, prepared promotions for the headline titles, and tracked which games sold, which ones we should push, and why some were slow. It was sales and marketing at the same time.

I was also the youngest on the team when Xbox Series X and Series S arrived, so I learned launch marketing and sales by taking part. In one quarter of 2020 I produced 157 product detail pages and thumbnails for digital titles, along with pop up pages and point of purchase displays for retail events.

The gift set

Halo Infinite was the headline game for the new consoles, so we made a Halo gift for customers who preordered a console. It was limited to 100 people.

I designed the goods and handled their production: a boxed set with a stainless mug, face masks with a strap, and a game item card.

Design decisions

  • The boxI first explored a deep sleeve box with a pull tab, then moved to a magnetic flip box with foam inserts so each item has its own place.
  • Quiet brandingBlack and white, with the Halo Infinite wordmark and the Master Chief helmet drawn in line. The key art appears once, on the lid.
  • Mask packagingEach mask sits on a printed card of game artwork, so opening it shows the world of the game first.

The sale page

I also designed the long product page announcing when Xbox Series X|S orders would open, with the two consoles side by side and the main features below.

What I took from it

This is where I learned that a launch plan lives in spreadsheets, but the launch itself lives in the things people see and hold. It is the reason I moved from marketing into design.
