Profile

In Korea I worked on the business side of well known brands. I learned how a launch is planned, what a budget allows, and how quickly an audience decides whether to care.

Design is where I want to use that. I work across graphic design, UI/UX and marketing, and I like projects where all three meet: a campaign that needs a poster, a product that needs a screen, a restaurant that needs a menu people enjoy reading.
